TORC Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

TORC is a personal health and fitness tracker. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: what you log stays on your phone, and we do not collect it. The app goes online only to look up foods and to manage an optional subscription, both described below.

What we collect

Nothing. TORC does not require an account, and we run no servers that receive your information. The things you log are not sent to us or to anyone else. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

What the app stores on your device

To work, TORC saves what you choose to enter, in a database that lives only on your phone:

  • Your profile and goals, such as height, weight, age, sex, and activity level.
  • Food, calorie, and macro logs.
  • Workouts, training plans, steps, sleep, mood, mindfulness, supplements, yoga and stretching, and sports sessions.
  • Body measurements and weight entries.
  • Cycle tracking entries, if you turn that on.
  • Progress photos, if you add them.
  • Your settings and dashboard layout.

Some of this is health information, which is sensitive. It stays on your device, under your control.

Permissions

  • Camera: used to scan food barcodes and to take progress photos. Images are processed on your device.
  • Photo library: used only when you choose a photo to add as a progress photo.
  • Notifications: used to show reminders you set up. These are created on your device.

You can change any of these at any time in your phone's Settings.

Food lookups (the only time TORC goes online)

When you search for a food by name or scan a barcode, TORC sends that search text or barcode number over the internet to a public food database so it can show you nutrition information:

  • Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) is used for name searches and barcodes.
  • USDA FoodData Central (api.nal.usda.gov) is used only if you enter your own free USDA key in Settings. The request includes that key.

These services can see the search text or barcode, your device's IP address, and the app's name and version, as with any internet request. TORC sends them nothing else: not your profile, goals, logs, or photos. We do not receive or store your searches. Each service has its own privacy policy, which applies to what it does with a request. Foods you look up are saved on your phone so they are quicker to find next time. If you do not search for foods online and do not use a subscription, the app makes no network requests.

Subscription and Trial

Usage of TORC requires a paid subsciption, billed monthly or yearly (at a discounted rate). Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, and we never see your payment details. To know whether your subscription is active, TORC uses a service called RevenueCat (revenuecat.com). RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status from Apple, and basic technical details such as your device type and IP address. It does not receive anything you log in TORC: no health data, profile, photos, or food entries. RevenueCat has its own privacy policy. We do not use it for advertising or tracking only for subsciption management.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, or give your information to anyone. Apart from the food lookups and subscription check described above, nothing leaves your phone. If you export or share a file from the app (for example, a backup), it goes where you choose to send it, and that is your decision.

Backups

Your phone's own backup, such as iCloud Backup, may include TORC's data. That is handled by Apple under your Apple account settings, not by TORC.

Deleting your data

Because the data lives only on your phone, deleting the app removes it. We have no copy and cannot recover it for you, so export a backup first if you want to keep your records.

Not medical advice

TORC is for general fitness and wellness tracking. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition, and calorie, macro, and training suggestions are estimates. Talk to a doctor or qualified professional before changing your diet or exercise routine, especially if you have a medical condition.

Children

TORC is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
